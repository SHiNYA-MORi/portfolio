Artworks

Under the collective identity "Yu Tokiwa" [Implemented by imi Inc.]

Lights (2025)

Sumida Mukojima EXPO 6 — Mist, light bulb, glass

An installation featuring a glass and a bulb salvaged from the venue house—representing past community life—juxtaposed with dynamically shifting mist generating fleeting, subjective rainbows. It invites viewers to imagine "invisible rainbows" that fall outside standard human sensory perception.

Flowers (2024)

Sumida Mukojima EXPO 2024 — Text, NFT, cube signage, natural sounds, floral scents

An interactive installation using text-only NFTs (built on the open-source "Loot" protocol) mapped to native flora of Kyojima (a historic pre-war neighborhood in Tokyo). Projected on cube screens with olfactory and ambient soundscapes, it triggers unique, subjective floral landscapes inside the viewers' minds.

Epistemology in AI (2024)

Winner of "We Love the Art" AI Art Category — AI-generated digital painting

A generative digital artwork exploring AI's "Umwelt" (subjective world). By inputting non-intentional human sketches and instructing the AI to interpret and render, the work reveals the AI's internal processing bias and autonomous visual grammar.

Production Projects

Hirofumi Uzawa 10th Anniversary Conference "Social Common Capital"

Sep 27, 2024 — United Nations University, Tokyo [Implemented by imi Inc.]

A major commemorative forum celebrating economist Hirofumi Uzawa (one of Japan's most prominent environmental economists, known as a leading candidate for the Nobel Prize in Economics). Focused on re-contextualizing his environmental and social framework "Social Common Capital" through the lens of modern internet, open-source software, and Web3. Attracted ~400 participants.

Role: Co-conceived and executed as Lead Producer / Project Manager / Creative Director / PR Director in partnership with Uzawa Memorial House.

"PLURALITY BOOK" Japan Release Launch

Official translation release event for the book "PLURALITY", hosting authors Audrey Tang and E. Glen Weyl with moderator Ryuichiro Takeshita.

Role: Organizer, booking agent, production lead, and program manager.
