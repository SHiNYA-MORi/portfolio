Shinya Mori is an artist and researcher exploring the co-existence of multiple realities through the intersection of technology, society, and art. He serves as the Director of "Yu Tokiwa," imi Inc.'s collective artist identity.

Since 2022, his primary research focus has been the sustainability of digital public goods. He specializes in the research, design, and social implementation of decentralized grants mechanisms that support open-source communities. He has presented his research at premier global venues, including the Ethereum Foundation's Devcon SEA, actively contributing to the international Ethereum-centric public goods ecosystem. As the co-organizer of "Funding the Commons Tokyo", he designed the event concepts, structured academic agendas, and secured global partnerships, building a collaborative platform for experts across industry, government, and academia.

In December 2023, he co-founded imi Inc. and established the artist group "Yu Tokiwa" to lead art direction and concept formulation with a focus on socio-technological implications. By bridging academic research, community-engaged social practice, and artistic creation, he explores new spatial, systemic, and relational models where diverse narratives can coexist.

Artworks

Under the collective identity "Yu Tokiwa" [Implemented by imi Inc.]

Lights (2025)

Sumida Mukojima EXPO 6 — Mist, light bulb, glass

An installation featuring a glass and a bulb salvaged from the venue house—representing past community life—juxtaposed with dynamically shifting mist generating fleeting, subjective rainbows. It invites viewers to imagine "invisible rainbows" that fall outside standard human sensory perception.

Flowers (2024)

Sumida Mukojima EXPO 2024 — Text, NFT, cube signage, natural sounds, floral scents

An interactive installation using text-only NFTs (built on the open-source "Loot" protocol) mapped to native flora of Kyojima (a historic pre-war neighborhood in Tokyo). Projected on cube screens with olfactory and ambient soundscapes, it triggers unique, subjective floral landscapes inside the viewers' minds.

Epistemology in AI (2024)

Winner of "We Love the Art" AI Art Category — AI-generated digital painting

A generative digital artwork exploring AI's "Umwelt" (subjective world). By inputting non-intentional human sketches and instructing the AI to interpret and render, the work reveals the AI's internal processing bias and autonomous visual grammar.

Research Projects & Systems

Nishiawakura Village Natural Capital App

2026–Present [Implemented by imi Inc.]

A mobile app and on-chain ecosystem designed in collaboration with Nishiawakura Village (Okayama Prefecture), Nishiawakura the Village-Wide Research Institute, and Code for Japan. It maps regional forest conservation and biodiversity initiatives to on-chain carbon credits, distributing tokenized incentives to contributors.

Role: Token economics architect, business developer, and marketing lead.

MUSE (Impact Evaluation System)

2025 [Co-developed by imi Inc. under R&D Contract with General Incorporated Association Beacon Labs]

An open-source protocol and framework evaluating qualitative social impact through evidence-based practice (EBP) in digital public goods, science, and arts. MUSE utilizes AI to construct logic models (causal pathways of impact) and dynamically anchors them to empirical research evidence.

Role: Co-initiator, concept formulator, specification architect, and R&D protocol lead.

Experimental Village "kuu village"

2025–Present [Implemented by imi Inc.]

A one-week resilience residency testing off-grid living across digital (self-hosted servers, onchain coordination protocol) and physical (DIY shelters, water wells, mutual support) domains to foster autonomous local community networks.

Role: Co-initiator & R&D experiment producer.

"Funding the Commons Tokyo" Symposium

2024

Japan's inaugural academic symposium addressing sustainable funding mechanisms for public goods, integrating Web3, cryptography, and alternative economics.

Role: Co-organizer. Contributed to concept design, curated academic agendas, and secured global partnerships with prominent international researchers.

Production Projects

Hirofumi Uzawa 10th Anniversary Conference "Social Common Capital"

Sep 27, 2024 — United Nations University, Tokyo [Implemented by imi Inc.]

A major commemorative forum celebrating economist Hirofumi Uzawa (one of Japan's most prominent environmental economists, known as a leading candidate for the Nobel Prize in Economics). Focused on re-contextualizing his environmental and social framework "Social Common Capital" through the lens of modern internet, open-source software, and Web3. Attracted ~400 participants.

Role: Co-conceived and executed as Lead Producer / Project Manager / Creative Director / PR Director in partnership with Uzawa Memorial House.

"PLURALITY BOOK" Japan Release Launch

May 2025 — Roppongi Tsutaya Books [Implemented by imi Inc.]

Official translation release event for the book "PLURALITY", hosting authors Audrey Tang and E. Glen Weyl with moderator Ryuichiro Takeshita.

Role: Organizer, booking agent, production lead, and program manager.
