Shinya Mori

Artist / Director of "Yu Tokiwa", Co-founder of imi Inc.

Shinya Mori is an artist and researcher exploring the co-existence of multiple realities through the intersection of technology, society, and art. He serves as the Director of "Yu Tokiwa," imi Inc.'s collective artist identity.

Since 2022, his primary research focus has been the sustainability of digital public goods. He specializes in the research, design, and social implementation of decentralized grants mechanisms that support open-source communities. He has presented his research at premier global venues, including the Ethereum Foundation's Devcon SEA, actively contributing to the international Ethereum-centric public goods ecosystem. As the co-organizer of "Funding the Commons Tokyo", he designed the event concepts, structured academic agendas, and secured global partnerships, building a collaborative platform for experts across industry, government, and academia.

In December 2023, he co-founded imi Inc. and established the artist group "Yu Tokiwa" to lead art direction and concept formulation with a focus on socio-technological implications. By bridging academic research, community-engaged social practice, and artistic creation, he explores new spatial, systemic, and relational models where diverse narratives can coexist.

Yu Tokiwa

Collective artist identity of imi Inc., established in 2023. Shinya Mori serves as Director.

2025

Lights

Sumida Mukojima EXPO 6 — Mist, light bulb, glass

An installation featuring a glass and a bulb salvaged from the venue house—representing past community life—juxtaposed with dynamically shifting mist generating fleeting, subjective rainbows. It invites viewers to imagine "invisible rainbows" that fall outside standard human sensory perception.

2024

Flowers

Sumida Mukojima EXPO 2024 — Text, NFT, cube signage, natural sounds, floral scents

An interactive installation using text-only NFTs (built on the open-source "Loot" protocol) mapped to native flora of Kyojima (a historic pre-war neighborhood in Tokyo). Projected on cube screens with olfactory and ambient soundscapes, it triggers unique, subjective floral landscapes inside the viewers' minds.

2024

Epistemology in AI

AI Art Category Winner, "We Love the Art" Digital Art Contest — AI-generated digital painting

A generative digital artwork exploring AI's "Umwelt" (subjective world). By inputting non-intentional human sketches and instructing the AI to interpret and render, the work reveals the AI's internal processing bias and autonomous visual grammar.

Selected Research Projects

2025–

Experimental Village "kuu village"

Co-initiator & R&D experiment producer

A one-week resilience residency testing off-grid living across digital (self-hosted servers, onchain coordination protocol) and physical (DIY shelters, water wells, mutual support) domains to foster autonomous local community networks.

2025

MUSE (Impact Evaluation System)

Co-initiator, concept formulator, specification architect, and R&D protocol lead

An open-source protocol and framework evaluating qualitative social impact through evidence-based practice (EBP) in digital public goods, science, and arts. MUSE utilizes AI to construct logic models (causal pathways of impact) and dynamically anchors them to empirical research evidence. Developed with Beacon Labs.
