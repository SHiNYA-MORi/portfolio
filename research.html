Selected Research Projects & Systems

Nishiawakura Village Natural Capital App

2026–Present [Implemented by imi Inc.]

A mobile app and on-chain ecosystem designed in collaboration with Nishiawakura Village (Okayama Prefecture), Nishiawakura the Village-Wide Research Institute, and Code for Japan. It maps regional forest conservation and biodiversity initiatives to on-chain carbon credits, distributing tokenized incentives to contributors.

Role: Token economics architect, business developer, and marketing lead.

MUSE (Impact Evaluation System)

2025 [Co-developed by imi Inc. under R&D Contract with General Incorporated Association Beacon Labs]

An open-source protocol and framework evaluating qualitative social impact through evidence-based practice (EBP) in digital public goods, science, and arts. MUSE utilizes AI to construct logic models (causal pathways of impact) and dynamically anchors them to empirical research evidence.

Role: Co-initiator, concept formulator, specification architect, and R&D protocol lead.

Experimental Village "kuu village"

2025–Present [Implemented by imi Inc.]

A one-week resilience residency testing off-grid living across digital (self-hosted servers, onchain coordination protocol) and physical (DIY shelters, water wells, mutual support) domains to foster autonomous local community networks.

Role: Co-initiator & R&D experiment producer.

"Funding the Commons Tokyo" Symposium

2024

Japan's inaugural academic symposium addressing sustainable funding mechanisms for public goods, integrating Web3, cryptography, and alternative economics.

Role: Co-organizer. Contributed to concept design, curated academic agendas, and secured global partnerships with prominent international researchers.
